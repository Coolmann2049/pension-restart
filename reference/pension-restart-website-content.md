## 1. Product position

Pension Restart is an elderly-first pension guidance and recovery service. It is designed for a pensioner whose payment has stopped, who does not know why, and who may not be comfortable navigating apps, portals, pension terminology or grievance forms.

The proposed service begins with a phone call. A voice guide listens to the pensioner's problem, turns it into a clear case summary, identifies the likely next step and tells the pensioner what information or documents may be needed. The same journey can be completed online by the pensioner or by a family member.

The submission prototype is deliberately deterministic:

- The call follows one scripted synthetic case.
- The transcript and extracted case details are prewritten.
- Document processing and human review are simulated.
- No live government portal is accessed.
- No Aadhaar, bank, OTP or other sensitive information is collected.
- The future AI voice service is demonstrated as a product concept, not represented as a live model.

### Primary problem

When a pension stops, the pensioner often cannot tell whether the cause is a missed life certificate, incorrect pension details, a rejected Digital Life Certificate, a bank-side delay or another administrative issue. The existing experience asks the pensioner to understand the system before the system will help them.

### Product correction

Pension Restart does not claim to issue or approve a Digital Life Certificate. A valid life certificate must still be completed through an eligible official route. The product provides understandable guidance, prepares the pensioner, records supporting information and keeps the next action visible.

### Section 5 — Life certificate explainer

Section label:

> UNDERSTAND THE REQUIREMENT

Headline:

> **What is a life certificate?**

Body:

> A life certificate confirms that the pensioner is alive and remains eligible for continued pension payments. Jeevan Pramaan is an Aadhaar-based Digital Life Certificate service. It is an additional digital route; eligible pensioners may also have conventional or assisted ways to submit a life certificate.

Three-step explainer:

1. **Prepare**  
   Keep the PPO number, pension account details, pension-sanctioning authority and pension-disbursing authority available.

2. **Complete proof of life**  
   Use an eligible official route such as the Jeevan Pramaan mobile application, an assisted centre, the pension-paying agency or an available doorstep service.

3. **Keep the acknowledgement**  
   Save the Pramaan ID or physical acknowledgement and check whether the pension authority accepted the certificate.

Important callout:

> Generating a Digital Life Certificate and receiving pension credit are two separate events. Keep proof of submission until the pension is credited normally.

### Section 6 — Ways to complete proof of life

Headline:

> **You do not have to solve it in only one way.**

#### Route A — Mobile face authentication

> Complete a Digital Life Certificate using a compatible phone, Jeevan Pramaan and AadhaarFaceRD. A trusted person may assist as the operator, but the pensioner must complete the required authentication.

Best for:

> Pensioners with a compatible phone and someone available to help.

#### Route B — Fingerprint or iris device

> Use the Jeevan Pramaan application with a supported registered biometric device and its RD Service.

Best for:

> Households or centres that already have a compatible biometric device.

#### Route C — Bank, post office or treasury

> Visit the pension-disbursing agency or another available assisted location with the required pension details.

Best for:

> Pensioners who prefer in-person assistance.

#### Route D — Citizen Service Centre

> A centre operator enters the pension information and assists with the Digital Life Certificate process.

Best for:

> Pensioners without suitable devices at home.

#### Route E — Doorstep service

> Where available, a postman, Gramin Dak Sevak or participating bank representative can assist at the pensioner's residence.

Best for:

> Older, infirm or mobility-limited pensioners.

#### Route F — Conventional life certificate

> Some pensioners can submit the prescribed certificate through an authorised official or their pension-disbursing agency instead of using Jeevan Pramaan.

Best for:

> Pensioners who are ineligible for the digital route or cannot complete Aadhaar-based authentication.

Disclaimer beneath the cards:

> Availability and rules depend on pension category, pension authority, bank and location. Pension Restart provides guidance; the relevant authority makes the final decision.

### Suggested FAQ answers

#### Can Pension Restart issue my life certificate?

> No. Pension Restart explains the available routes and helps you prepare. The official life-certificate process must be completed through an eligible government, pension-authority, bank, post-office, centre or approved doorstep channel.

#### Is the displayed phone number active?

> No. It is a prototype number used to demonstrate the proposed call-first experience. Select “Simulate a call” to view the working scripted journey.

#### Is the voice assistant using AI in this prototype?

> No. The prototype uses a deterministic scripted conversation and prewritten case extraction. A production version could use a multilingual voice model with human oversight.

#### Are my documents sent anywhere?

> No. The submission prototype accepts only supplied synthetic files and does not send them to a government system.

#### Does “documents received” mean the pension will restart?

> No. It only means that the demonstration files were attached to the synthetic record. The pension authority must accept the life certificate, and the pension-paying institution must credit the pension.

#### Can a family member complete everything?

> A trusted person can prepare information and help operate eligible tools, but the pensioner must complete required authentication or personal declarations.

## 15. About and disclosure page

### About Pension Restart

> Pension Restart began with a real family experience: an elderly relative's pension stopped because nobody knew that her life certificate was due. The product asks a simple question—what if the pensioner could begin with one phone call instead of first understanding the machinery of pension administration?

### What this prototype changes

> It reorganises the journey around the pensioner's situation: “my pension stopped.” It then explains the likely paths, records the evidence and makes the next action understandable to both the pensioner and a trusted helper.

### What this prototype does not do

> It does not authenticate identity, generate a real Digital Life Certificate, contact a bank, access a government portal, make an eligibility decision or cause pension to be credited.

### How Codex was used

Draft placeholder to update after implementation:

> Codex was used throughout the build to research the existing journey, translate policy constraints into product states, create and refine the responsive implementation, generate deterministic demo fixtures, test accessibility and interaction states, and document which dependencies and integrations are simulated.

### Data statement

> All names, references, documents, dates and account information shown in the prototype are synthetic. Do not enter real Aadhaar, PAN, OTP, payment, health or banking information.

### Independence statement

> Pension Restart is an independent hackathon prototype. It is not affiliated with, approved by or operated by Jeevan Pramaan, UIDAI, EPFO, a bank, a post office or any government department.

## 18. Hardcoded prototype behaviour

### Resettable global fixture

The prototype should ship with a visible “Reset demonstration” action on the disclosure page and an unobtrusive reset action available to the presenter.

### Deterministic transitions

```text
Start simulated call
→ play scripted conversation
→ show timed transcript
→ display hardcoded parsed summary
→ confirm summary
→ request three predefined document types
→ attach two bundled synthetic files
→ show documents-received confirmation
→ open case page
→ use explicit demo control to simulate review
→ show assisted-route recommendation
→ optionally record synthetic Pramaan ID
→ optionally show acceptance and pension-protected end state
```

### Important implementation labels

Use:

- Scripted call
- Demonstration extraction
- Synthetic document
- Simulated review
- Mock official status
- User-confirmed pension credit

Do not use:

- Live AI analysis
- Submitted to Jeevan Pramaan
- Government verified
- Fast-tracked
- Officially approved
- Pension restored

unless the phrase is visibly qualified as a simulated future state.

## 19. Resend decision

Recommendation: **Do not buy a domain solely for Resend before the core UI is finished.**

Resend adds a small amount of credibility, but it is not the product insight. The most valuable first-minute demonstration is:

1. Simulated elderly call
2. Immediate structured summary
3. Requested document upload
4. Documents-received state
5. Clear next route

Implement the email templates visually inside the website first. If the responsive journey, accessibility states, reset behaviour and video are complete with time remaining, connect Resend to one controlled demo inbox. Describe it as a working notification channel, not a government update.
